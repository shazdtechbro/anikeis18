My Anike. ❤️

My Anike. My Ejeh mi. ❤️

Before you say “awn” and move on, come back small. 😹 I know I can be a lot with all these sweet words. Maybe you are even tired of hearing me say how much I love you. But this one, read it slowly. You do not have to find a perfect reply. I just want you to know what is in my heart.

I sincerely love you, Shukroh. Underneath the teasing, the “Her Majesty,” the Mrs Sanni agenda, and all my small small wahala, that is the truth. I am so, so rooting for you.

I want to be one of the best things that ever happened to you. And I know saying it is the easy part. I want to show you through how I listen, how I support you, how I keep my word, and how I stay kind on the ordinary days too. On 27 May, I told you, “I would be patient 😚”. You said, “I would so much love that”. I still mean it.

I want to support you and give you room to spread your wings. Your dreams do not have to become smaller because you are loved by me. I want to see you grow into all the good things you hope to become. Nurse Olaiya, I want to be there cheering, helping where I can, and reminding you of yourself when a hard day makes you forget.

May Allah ease your clinical postings, your exams, and the days you come home tired. May none of your effort go in vain. May He grant you beneficial knowledge, a graduation with flying colours, and the first class we are praying for. May you have the strength, support, and peace you need along the way. Ameen. 🤲🏾

I kept thinking about what to give you. What could hold all this love properly? Money alone could not say everything I wanted to say. A gift could be lovely, but I wanted something you could come back to and find us inside it. Our smiles. Our places. Our words. The little things that make you, you.

So I chose this. A little world with your name all over it. I hope it makes you smile, and I hope you feel the care inside it. It does not have to be the only thing. You can still tell your boy what you would like, ma. Chocolate has not been cancelled. 😹🍫❤️

I do not want my love to feel like another thing you have to manage. You do not owe me an “awn,” tears, or a big reaction. I want you to feel loved, supported, and free to be yourself with me. Even when you are tired. Even when you have no sweet words left.

By the special grace of Allah, we are going to win in life together. We still have things to figure out, work to do, and dreams to grow into. But I love that we found us already. That part means so much to me.

And if Allah blesses us with marriage, I pray we build a home where that love feels real every day. Patience. Laughter. Someone to tell your day to. Two people who keep choosing kindness and making room for each other. May He make us good for one another and ease our path to becoming husband and wife. Ameen, bi’idhnillah. ❤️

Maldives next, maybe? After the wedding, in sha Allah. You once asked if our Maldives money was almost complete. Her Majesty, the agenda is still alive. We just need to make it. Your fine boy is ready for the blue water. 🤭😹

Life has so much good waiting for us, bi’idhnillah. I want to work towards it with you. Not just dream about the big moments, but care for the little ones too.

Happy birthday, omo oni birthday. May Allah bless you for me, put barakah in everything good you do, and keep giving you reasons to smile like the girl in these photographs. You are loved, Anike. Sincerely. ❤️
